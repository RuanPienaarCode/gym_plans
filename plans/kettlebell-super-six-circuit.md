KETTLEBELL SUPER SIX CIRCUIT. Difficulty 2 of 3. Six movement patterns in
one circuit: a hinge (swing), an upper-body push (floor press), a squat
(front squat), an upper-body pull (renegade row), an isolation move (curl)
and core (leg sway).

HOW TO RUN IT. Five rounds at every level. Rest 30 seconds between
exercises, and only as long as you need between rounds. Warm up first with a
few minutes of easy movement, a Kettlebell Halo and some light swings. The
six patterns are what matter, so swap in your own version of any of them:
Goblet Squat for the front squat, Kettlebell Row for the renegade row.

## Super Six Circuit (any)

- Kettlebell Swing | 5 x 10
- Kettlebell Floor Press | 5 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 5 x 8/side
- Renegade Row | 5 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Curl | 5 x 10
- Kettlebell Leg Sway | 5 x 10
