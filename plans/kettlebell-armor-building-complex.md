THE ARMOR BUILDING COMPLEX — Dan John's, and the most copied kettlebell
complex on the internet. One "rep" of the ABC is:

  2 cleans → 1 press → 3 front squats

Do that, put the bell down, breathe, do it again. It is the closest thing a
single kettlebell gives you to a hypertrophy session: the clean loads the
hinge, the press loads the shoulder, and by the third front squat your whole
torso is holding on. Ten rounds is a real workout in about fifteen minutes.

THE THREE LINES IN EACH DAY BELOW ARE ONE COMPLEX, NOT THREE EXERCISES. Do not
do ten sets of cleans and then ten sets of presses — that is a different and
much easier workout. Read them as: clean, clean, press, squat, squat, squat,
set the bell down.

WITH ONE BELL, do the complex on one side, rest, then the other. The original
is written for two bells; one bell just means twice the rounds and half the
load per round, and it works.

HOW TO RUN IT. Two ways, both popular:

- EMOM — start a complex at the top of every minute and rest whatever is
  left. Self-limiting, and it stops you rushing.
- Rounds — complex, rest until your breathing settles, repeat. Better when
  the bell is heavy or the day is not going well.

Start at five rounds a side and build to ten before you touch a heavier bell.
The press is always the weak link, so pick the bell your PRESS can handle, not
the one your squat could.

Twice a week is plenty alongside anything else. Three times is a programme in
its own right and nothing else should be competing with it.

## Complex + Swings (mon)

Ten rounds. Clean, clean, press, three front squats — then swings to finish.

- Kettlebell Clean | 10 x 2/side
- Kettlebell Press | 10 x 1/side
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 10 x 3/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 5 x 20

## Complex · Short (wed)

Five rounds, run as an EMOM if you want a clock to keep you honest. Get-ups
close it out slowly.

- Kettlebell Clean | 5 x 2/side
- Kettlebell Press | 5 x 1/side
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 5 x 3/side
- Turkish Get-Up | 5 x 1/side

## Complex + Swings (fri)

Ten rounds again, heavier swings. This is the session to add a round to when
the bell starts feeling easy.

- Kettlebell Clean | 10 x 2/side
- Kettlebell Press | 10 x 1/side
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 10 x 3/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 10 x 20
