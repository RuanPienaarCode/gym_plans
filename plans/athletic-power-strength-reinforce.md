AN ATHLETIC WEEK in the format university and club strength programmes use:
three different days, each one a short activation followed by blocks you
SUPERSET. Power on Monday while you are fresh, Strength on Wednesday,
Reinforce on Friday. It trains you to jump, land, push, pull and run — not
just to lift — and it leaves Tuesday and Saturday clear for running.

READ EACH DAY AS BLOCKS. The blocks are named at the top of every day and
the exercises are listed in block order. One set of each exercise in the
block, rest 60–90 seconds (two full minutes on Power), round again until the
block is done. Every block pairs things that do not compete — a leg move
with an upper-body one, a big lift with trunk or tendon work — which is why
a superset costs no quality.

THE THREE DAYS, AND WHY IN THIS ORDER:

- POWER (mon). Jumps, throws and explosive pulls. Speed is the whole point,
  so it comes first in the week, after a rest day, and every rep is maximal
  intent with full rest. Low reps on purpose: the moment the jumps get lower,
  the set is over.
- STRENGTH (wed). Split squats, the hinge, pull-ups and pressing, in the 5–8
  rep range. Leave one or two reps in the tank. It sits mid-week so the legs
  have 72 hours before a weekend long run.
- REINFORCE (fri). Tendons, ankles, glute med and trunk. Light on the
  nervous system, so it is safe the day before a long run. It is also
  available on its own as Athletic · Reinforce Day.

RAMP THE EXPLOSIVE WORK. Tendons adapt slower than muscle, and slower again
with age. For the first two weeks use a low box, skip the lateral bounds and
do the Power day at half the sets. Landings over height, always: quiet,
knees over toes, step down from the box.

KIT: a pull-up bar, dumbbells, a kettlebell, a box or step, a long band and a
mini band. The landmine press and the chest pass want a barbell and a
medicine ball; their exercise notes name home swaps.

## Power (mon)

Warm-up · Activate — World's Greatest Stretch, then pogo hops for the ankles.
Block 1 · Lower Power + Upper Pull Power — box jumps with explosive high
pull-ups. Two full minutes between rounds.
Block 2 · Hip Power + Horizontal Push Power — swings with explosive push-ups.
Block 3 · Lateral Power + Trunk — bounds you stick, with the swinging Pallof.
Block 4 · Tendon Tolerance + Calves — Spanish squat holds with straight-leg
calf raises.

- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 4/side
- Pogo Hops | 2 x 15
- Box Jumps | 4 x 3
- Explosive High Pull-ups | 4 x 3
- Kettlebell Swing | 3 x 10
- Explosive Push-ups | 3 x 5
- Lateral Bounds | 3 x 4/side
- Dynamic Pallof Press | 3 x 10/side
- Spanish Squat | 3 x 30-45s hold
- Single-leg Calf Raise | 3 x 12/side

## Strength (wed)

Warm-up · Activate & Stabilise — bear plank to inchworm, band pull-aparts.
Block 1 · Knee-dominant + Vertical Pull — split squats with pull-ups.
Block 2 · Hip-dominant + Horizontal Push — Romanian deadlift with floor press.
Keep the RDL moderate; stop the set the moment the back rounds.
Block 3 · Horizontal Pull + Trunk — rows with farmer's carries.
Block 4 · Tendon Tolerance + Knee Control — slow step-downs with KOT calf
raises.

- Bear Plank to Inchworm & T-spine Rotation | 2 x 6/side
- Band Pull-Aparts | 2 x 15
- Bulgarian Split Squat | 4 x 6-8/leg
- Pull-ups | 4 x 5
- Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 8
- Dumbbell Floor Press | 3 x 8
- One-arm Dumbbell Row | 3 x 10/side
- Farmer's Carry | 3 x 40s
- Eccentric Step-down | 3 x 8/leg
- Single-leg KOT Calf Raise | 3 x 12/side

## Reinforce (fri)

Warm-up · Activate & Stabilise — bear plank to inchworm + t-spine rotation.
Block 1 · OH Strength + Horizontal Upper-body Power — superset.
Block 2 · Trunk + Glute Med + Calves — tri-set. The Pallof press uses a
kettlebell tied to the band, so the load swings.
Block 3 · Tendon Tolerance + Ankle Protection — four in a row. Spanish squat:
your body should look like it is in a wall sit.

- Bear Plank to Inchworm & T-spine Rotation | 2 x 6/side
- Single-arm Landmine Push Press | 3 x 8/arm
- Lying Chest Pass | 3 x 8
- Dynamic Pallof Press | 3 x 10/side
- Captain Morgan | 3 x 12/side
- Single-leg KOT Calf Raise | 3 x 15/side
- Spanish Squat | 3 x 10
- Banded Ankle Dorsiflexion | 3 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Plantar Flexion | 3 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Inversion & Eversion | 3 x 15/side
