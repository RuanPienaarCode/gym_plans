RITE OF PASSAGE — the programme from Pavel Tsatsouline's Enter the Kettlebell,
and the classic answer to "I have one bell and I want to get STRONG rather
than just conditioned". Two lifts carry it: the clean and press, and the
pull-up. Everything else is swings.

THE LADDER IS THE WHOLE IDEA. A ladder is 1 rep, rest, 2 reps, rest, 3 reps —
that is one ladder, six reps total. You do several ladders in a session. It
gets you a lot of quality reps at a weight you could never do for straight
sets of five, because you are always starting a rung fresh. Reps stay easy;
volume does the work.

THREE PRESS DAYS, DELIBERATELY UNEQUAL:

- MEDIUM day — 3 ladders to 3 rungs (1,2,3 × 3).
- LIGHT day — 2 ladders to 3 rungs. This day is meant to feel too easy. It
  is the reason the heavy day works, and it is the day people wrongly "fix".
- HEAVY day — 4 or 5 ladders to 3 rungs, and the only day you push.

When all five ladders on the heavy day feel strong, add a rung (to 4) before
you add a bell. Pull-ups ladder alongside the press, rung for rung — if you
cannot yet do five clean pull-ups, ladder Inverted Rows instead and come back.

THE VARIETY DAY is the fun one: swings, get-ups, and snatches if you have
them. It is not a fourth press day and should never become one.

SHOULDER WATCH-OUT. This programme puts a heavy bell overhead three times a
week, which is a lot of pressing for a shoulder with any nerve or impingement
history. The light day exists partly for that reason — take it seriously.
Sharp pain or anything travelling down the arm ends the exercise for the day,
and if pressing overhead is not clean on a given day, do the swings and go
home. There is no version of a strength programme that is worth a shoulder.

## Ladders · Medium (mon)

Clean each press rep fresh. Rest as long as you need between rungs — this is
strength work, not conditioning.

- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 3 x ladder 1-2-3/side
- Pull-ups | 3 x ladder 1-2-3
- Kettlebell Swing | 5 x 20

## Ladders · Light (wed)

The easy day. Do NOT add a ladder because you feel good — feeling good on
Wednesday is exactly what makes Friday possible.

- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 2 x ladder 1-2-3/side
- Pull-ups | 2 x ladder 1-2-3
- Kettlebell Swing | 5 x 20

## Ladders · Heavy (fri)

The day the programme actually progresses. Five ladders when the week has gone
well, four when it has not.

- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 5 x ladder 1-2-3/side
- Pull-ups | 5 x ladder 1-2-3
- Kettlebell Swing | 10 x 20

## Variety Day (sat)

Not a press day. Pick what you enjoy from below, keep it under twenty minutes,
and finish fresh. Skip the snatches entirely if you have not been coached on
them — the swings do the same job.

- Kettlebell Swing | 10 x 20
- Turkish Get-Up | 5 x 1/side
- Kettlebell Snatch | 5 x 5/side optional
- Kettlebell Windmill | 2 x 5/side
