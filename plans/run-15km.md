TWELVE WEEKS TO 15KM ON THE ROAD. Three runs a week — easy, tempo, long —
with the long one climbing the ladder and stepping down in weeks 4 and 8.

THE LADDER, in kilometres: 5, 6, 7, 6, 8, 9, 10, 8, 11, 12.5, 14, 15. Two
step-downs, both deliberate. Repeat a week whenever it felt hard rather than
pushing through to the next number; the ladder is a guide, not a contract,
and 15km run comfortably in week 14 beats 15km survived in week 12.

THE TEMPO IS THE ENGINE at this distance. Fifteen kilometres is far enough
that pure easy running stops being enough on its own, and short enough that
you do not need the huge weekly volume a half marathon asks for. One
comfortably-hard run a week is the difference.

TRAIL OR ROAD. This is the road-shaped sibling of Trail Base — Road to 15km.
The difference is not the distance, it is that this one is a MAIN plan that
drives your week, while Trail Base runs parallel to a strength programme and
walks the climbs. Pick by what the rest of your week already looks like.

## Easy Run (tue)

Thirty minutes conversational. Treadmill, road, whatever gets it done.

- Easy Run | 1 x 30 min easy

## Tempo (thu)

Ten easy, the tempo block, ten easy. Comfortably hard means you can speak a
short sentence — no more.

- Tempo Run | 1 x 4-6 km at tempo

## Long Run (sat)

The ladder run. Slow, and finish feeling like there was another kilometre in
you.

- Easy Run | 1 x per the ladder

## Recovery (sun)

- Recovery Walk | 1 x 30-40 min easy
