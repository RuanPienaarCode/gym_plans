DAN JOHN'S 10,000 SWING CHALLENGE — twenty workouts of 500 swings. It is a
month-long conditioning block with a cult following, and it is exactly what it
sounds like: brutally simple, genuinely effective, and not something to run
back to back.

ONE WORKOUT IS FIVE ROUNDS OF THIS:

  10 swings → strength lift → 15 swings → strength lift →
  25 swings → strength lift → 50 swings → rest

That is 100 swings a round and 500 a session. The strength lift between swing
sets is the "off-set" — one easy set of about five reps, never taken near
failure, and never done after the set of fifty. Its job is to give the hips a
breather while something useful happens, not to be a second workout.

THE PATTERN BELOW IS ONE ROUND. Do it five times. Written out five times it
would be twenty lines of the same thing and you would stop reading.

FOUR SESSIONS A WEEK FOR FIVE WEEKS gets you the twenty workouts. The original
runs five a week for four; four a week is the version that survives also
having a life, and the swings are the point either way.

WHAT IT IS AND IS NOT. It builds work capacity, a hard hinge and a very honest
relationship with your grip. It is not a hypertrophy programme and it has
almost no pressing or pulling volume — that is what the off-set lift is
patching, and it is a patch. One month, then move on.

CHOOSE THE BELL HONESTLY. Dan John's numbers assume a bell you can swing well
for fifty unbroken reps at the END of a session, not at the start. If the last
set of fifty turns into a back-led lift rather than a hip snap, the weight is
wrong — and 10,000 repetitions of a mediocre hinge is a lot of chances to find
out the hard way. Grip goes before the hips do; drop the bell rather than
hanging on with a failing hand.

## Swings · Press day (mon)

Round pattern below, five times through. Press is the off-set lift — five easy
reps a side, no grinding.

- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 15
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 25
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 50

## Swings · Squat day (tue)

Same round, five times through, with the front squat as the off-set. Legs will
already be warm; keep the squats light.

- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 15
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 25
- Kettlebell Front Squat | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 50

## Swings · Pull day (thu)

Rows as the off-set. This is the day that keeps the month from being all hinge
and no back.

- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Row | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 15
- Kettlebell Row | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 25
- Kettlebell Row | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 50

## Swings · Press day (fri)

Last of the week. If the hinge has gone ragged by Friday, cut to three rounds
and keep the form — a missed hundred swings costs nothing next to a back.

- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 15
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 25
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 50
