Every stretching and mobility session in this library, in one place. Not a
workout, and not the same thing as Rest & Recovery — that plan fills the empty
days automatically and asks nothing of you. These you choose, and they are
aimed at three specific places: the hips and ITB, the lower back, and the
shoulders. Those three are where most training histories keep their scars.

SIX SESSIONS, NONE WITH A WEEKDAY. Start whichever one you need from the
Plans page:

- FULL PASS — twenty minutes, top to bottom.
- 10-MINUTE RESET — the desk antidote.
- AFTER THE RUN — the ten minutes after a run, while everything is warm.
- LEGS & HIPS, SHOULDERS & ARMS, BACK & SPINE — targeted, for the day one
  thing in particular is tight.

WHERE TO PUT IT. A long hold leaves the muscle temporarily weaker and less
springy, which is a non-issue on a rest day and a real one twenty minutes
before heavy squats or intervals. So: after a session, after a run, on a rest
day, or in the evening — not as a warm-up. The warm-up before lifting or
running wants movement (World's Greatest Stretch, Inch Worms, Shoulder
Circles, a few Strides), not long holds.

WHAT IT DOES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT. Holds of 30 to 60 seconds, repeated,
several times a week, reliably build range — the gains come from accumulated
weekly time on a muscle rather than from any single heroic stretch. What the
evidence does NOT support is the old promise that stretching prevents injury
or meaningfully reduces next-day soreness. Do it because tight hips and calves
make training feel worse and the range is worth having. For keeping a back,
an ITB or a shoulder out of trouble, STRENGTH is the better-supported tool —
that is what Prehab · Back, Hips & Shoulders is for.

Breathe out into each hold and stop at mild tension. Nothing here should be
sharp, and nothing here should leave you sore tomorrow. Sharp or radiating
pain is a physiotherapist's problem, not a stretching plan's.

## Mobility · Full Pass (any)

About twenty minutes, top to bottom. Skip anything that feels fine and spend
the time on what does not.

- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 5/side
- Inch Worms | 1 x 8
- Cat-Cow | 1 x 10 slow
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Hamstring Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- IT Band and Glute Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Piriformis Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Calf Stretch | 1 x 30s each position
- Spinal Twist | 1 x 45s/side
- Shoulder Circles | 1 x 10/direction
- Band Pull-Aparts | 1 x 20
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s
- Child's Pose | 1 x 60s

## Mobility · 10-minute Reset (any)

The desk antidote. Hips open, back moving, shoulders back, breathing slow.
This is the one that actually gets done on a weekday evening.

- Cat-Cow | 1 x 8 slow
- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 4/side
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Band Pull-Aparts | 1 x 20
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30s
- Diaphragmatic Breathing | 1 x 8 slow breaths

## After the Run (any)

Ten minutes, in the kit you ran in, before you sit down. Calves and hips
first — they are what running actually shortens.

- Calf Stretch | 1 x 30s each position
- Hamstring Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- IT Band and Glute Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Piriformis Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Child's Pose | 1 x 60s

## Legs & Hips (any)

The longer version, for a rest day. If an ITB has ever been a problem, the
glute and piriformis holds are the two to give extra time — the band itself
does not stretch, the muscles pulling on it do.

- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 5/side
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 60s/side
- Hamstring Stretch | 1 x 60s/side
- IT Band and Glute Stretch | 1 x 60s/side
- Piriformis Stretch | 1 x 60s/side
- Calf Stretch | 1 x 45s each position
- Spinal Twist | 1 x 45s/side

## Shoulders & Arms (any)

For the day after pressing, or the day a desk has had you folded over a
keyboard. The band work is not a stretch — it is the small pulling volume
that keeps the joint happy, and it belongs here.

- Shoulder Circles | 1 x 10/direction
- Band Pull-Aparts | 2 x 15-20
- Band Face Pulls | 2 x 15-20
- Kettlebell Halo | 1 x 8/direction
- Dead Hang | 2 x 30s
- Child's Pose | 1 x 60s

## Back & Spine (any)

Movement first, holds second, breathing last. For a lower back that has
tightened up rather than one that hurts.

- Cat-Cow | 1 x 10 slow
- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 5/side
- Spinal Twist | 1 x 60s/side
- Piriformis Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s
- Child's Pose | 1 x 60s
- Diaphragmatic Breathing | 1 x 8 slow breaths
