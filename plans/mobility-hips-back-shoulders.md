Not a workout, and not the same thing as Rest & Recovery — that plan fills the
empty days automatically and asks nothing of you. This one you choose, and it
is aimed at three specific places: the hips and ITB, the lower back, and the
shoulders. Those three are where most training histories keep their scars, and
they are the three that decide whether the next twelve weeks go well.

WHERE TO PUT IT. Stretching leaves the muscle temporarily weaker and less
springy for a while afterward, which is a non-issue on a rest day and a real
one twenty minutes before heavy squats. So: after a session, on a rest day, or
in the evening — not as a warm-up. The warm-up before lifting wants movement
(World's Greatest Stretch, Inch Worms, Shoulder Circles), not long holds,
which is why a good lifting warm-up looks nothing like the list below.

WHAT ACTUALLY BUILDS RANGE. Holds of 30 to 60 seconds, repeated, several times
a week — the range gains come from accumulated weekly time on a muscle rather
than from any single heroic stretch. Consistency beats intensity here more
clearly than anywhere else in training. Two ten-minute passes a week will do
more than one punishing half hour a month.

Breathe out into the stretch and stop at mild tension, not pain. Nothing here
should be sharp, and nothing here should leave you sore tomorrow.

## Mobility · Full Pass (any)

About twenty minutes, top to bottom. Skip anything that feels fine and spend
the time on what does not.

- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 5/side
- Inch Worms | 1 x 8
- Cat-Cow | 1 x 10 slow
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Hamstring Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- IT Band and Glute Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Piriformis Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Calf Stretch | 1 x 30s each position
- Spinal Twist | 1 x 45s/side
- Shoulder Circles | 1 x 10/direction
- Band Pull-Aparts | 1 x 20
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s
- Child's Pose | 1 x 60s

## Mobility · 10-minute Reset (any)

The desk antidote. Hips open, back moving, shoulders back, breathing slow.
This is the one that actually gets done on a weekday evening.

- Cat-Cow | 1 x 8 slow
- World's Greatest Stretch | 1 x 4/side
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch | 1 x 45s/side
- Band Pull-Aparts | 1 x 20
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30s
- Diaphragmatic Breathing | 1 x 8 slow breaths
