ADVANCED KETTLEBELL FULL BODY. Difficulty 3 of 3. A hard full-body strength
session with one kettlebell. The standing strength work comes first, while
you are fresh, with a goblet squat added so the legs get more than the
swing. Rotation and core are on the floor at the end.

IF YOUR BACK OR SHOULDERS COMPLAIN: press a bell you control strictly, and
leave out Kettlebell Russian Twist and Kettlebell Weighted Sit-Up first.

HOW TO RUN IT. Start it as a timed session and set the clock for your level:
10 minutes for beginners, 20 for intermediate, 25 for advanced. Each station
gets the default 45 seconds plus a short changeover, and the list repeats
until the time runs out. The rep numbers set the pace; you don't have to hit
them. The order is built so that the first ten stations make a complete
session on their own (swing, clean and press, squat, row, press, figure 8, push-pull and punches), so a short clock never leaves out a whole
body part. Prefer counting reps? Work down the list once per round instead,
resting as long as you need between rounds.

## Advanced Full Body (any)

- Hand-to-Hand Kettlebell Swing | 1 x 16
- Kettlebell Clean and Press | 1 x 5/side
- Goblet Squat | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Row | 1 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Press | 1 x 6/side
- Kettlebell Figure 8 Pass | 1 x 8
- Kettlebell Push Pull Standing | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Pass Punches | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Double Reach | 1 x 8
- Kettlebell Leg Sway | 1 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Russian Twist | 1 x 10/side
- Kettlebell Weighted Sit-Up | 1 x 8
