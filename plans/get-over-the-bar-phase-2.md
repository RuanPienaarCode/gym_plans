PHASE 2 — THE MUSCLE-UP ITSELF. Phase 1 (Get Over The Bar) builds the pull-up
and the dip. This one teaches the part neither of them trains: the transition,
where the chest goes from below the bar to above it.

THE GATE. Start this when you have TEN clean pull-ups and TEN clean
straight-bar dips — or eight and eight, with explosive high pull-ups that
reach the lower chest. Earlier than that, the skill work just becomes more
tired pull-ups, and the elbows pay for it.

A MUSCLE-UP IS FOUR PIECES, and each day trains them in this order:

- THE GRIP. A false grip puts the wrist over the bar before the rep starts,
  so it does not have to roll over mid-rep. False Grip Hang builds it.
- THE HIGH PULL. A muscle-up needs the bar at the lower chest or belly, not
  the chin. Explosive High Pull-ups are that pull.
- THE TRANSITION. Elbows travel from below the bar to above it. Negative
  Muscle-ups teach the path slowly, while you are strong enough to control it.
- THE LOCKOUT. The rep ends in the bottom of a straight-bar dip and presses
  out. Deep dips are that finish.

SKILL FIRST, STRENGTH SECOND. The skill work opens every pull and push day
while you are fresh — a transition practised tired is a transition learned
wrong. Attempts are capped: three to five honest tries, then stop. A kipping,
one-arm-at-a-time "chicken wing" rep is not a muscle-up, and it is the rep
that hurts shoulders.

ELBOWS AND WRISTS ARE THE LIMIT. The false grip is hard on the wrists and the
transition is hard on the elbows. Build the hang time slowly, and any sharp
or pinpoint pain at a joint ends that exercise for the day. Muscle burn is
fine; joint pain is the warning.

THE WEEK keeps Phase 1's shape: Mon pull, Wed push, Thu legs & core, with
running on Tue and Sat and two clear rest days. Same five-minute warm-up as
Phase 1, plus two 15-second false grip hangs.

## A · Grip, High Pull & Transition (mon)

Skill first. Rest a full two minutes between the explosive and negative
sets — these are about quality, not fatigue.

- False Grip Hang | 3 x 15-30s
- Explosive High Pull-ups | 5 x 3
- Negative Muscle-ups | 4 x 2
- Pull-ups | 3 x submax
- Inverted Rows | 3 x 8-12
- Box Jumps | 3 x 8

## B · Attempts & Lockout (wed)

Attempts first: band-assisted until the transition is smooth, then
unassisted. Stop after five tries or the first ugly one. Then the dips —
deeper than in Phase 1, built up gradually; if anything tingles down the
arm, go back to parallel.

- Muscle-ups | 5 x 1 attempt
- Straight-bar Dips | 4 x 8-12
- Push-ups | 3 x 10-15
- Band Face Pulls | 3 x 15-20
- Hollow Hold | 3 x 20-30s

## C · Legs, Hinge & Core (thu)

Unchanged from Phase 1 — the legs do not care which phase the bar work is in.
Keep the dragon flag tucked until the L-sit holds for 15 seconds.

- Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 8-10
- Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 x 8/leg
- Pistol Squat Progression | 3 x 3-5/leg
- Toes-to-bar Ladder | 4 x 6-10
- L-sit & Dragon Flag | 3 x holds
