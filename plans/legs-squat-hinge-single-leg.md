A standalone leg session for the days Get Over The Bar's Thursday does not
cover, or for a week where legs got skipped. No weekday, on purpose — YOU
decide where it lands, and the placement matters more here than in any other
plan on this list.

NEVER THE DAY BEFORE THE LONG RUN. That rule is already written into Get Over
The Bar and it applies double to this session, which is heavier than the
Thursday it replaces. A leg day on Friday followed by a Saturday trail run is
exactly how an ITB gets angry. Give it 48 hours of clearance either side of
the long run.

Single-leg work leads the accessories on purpose. The taller you are the
longer the femurs, and a deep two-legged squat then asks a lot of the lower
back before it asks much of the quads; split squats and step-ups get the same job done with the
torso closer to upright. Load them with dumbbells at your sides rather than
overhead — the spine carries less that way.

If the front knee complains in a lunge, reverse it. The Reverse Lunge is in
here rather than the forward version for exactly that reason.

## Legs · Full Session (any)

Roughly 30 minutes. Rest ~2 minutes after the squat and the hinge — those two
are the session; the rest is support. Everything stops 2 reps short.

- Goblet Squat | 4 x 8-10
- Romanian Deadlift | 4 x 8-10
- Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 x 8/leg
- Reverse Lunge | 3 x 10/leg
- Single-leg Glute Bridge | 3 x 10/side
- Single-leg Calf Raise | 3 x 12/side
- Wall Sit | 2 x 45-60s

## Legs · 15-minute Express (any)

Bodyweight and one bell. For a travel week, a hotel room, or the evening the
full session is not happening.

- Bodyweight Squat | 3 x 20
- Forward Lunges | 3 x 20
- Kettlebell Deadlift | 3 x 12
- Calf Raises | 3 x 15
- Up Down Squat | 2 x 45s
