FIFTEEN MINUTES, TWICE A WEEK, for the three places most training histories
keep their scars: the lower back, the hips and ITB, and the shoulders. This
is strength work, not stretching — and that is the point of it.

WHY STRENGTH RATHER THAN STRETCHING:

- THE ITB does not stretch; it is a band of connective tissue. ITB trouble in
  runners is usually a hip that lets the knee drift inward on every stride,
  and the standard physiotherapy answer is hip abductor STRENGTH — side-lying
  abduction, lateral band walks, single-leg work.
- THE LOWER BACK does better with trunk ENDURANCE than with more range: the
  "McGill Big 3" (curl-up, side plank, bird dog) trains the muscles that hold
  the spine steady, in positions that do not repeatedly bend it.
- THE SHOULDER gets pressed in every push-up, dip and press you do. The
  external rotators and rear delts are what balance that — small muscles,
  light bands, slow reps.

THE BIG 3 ARE HOLDS, NOT REPS. Each rep is a 10-second hold with a breath or
two inside it. Do them as a descending ladder — five reps, then three, then
one with a short rest between — or simply three sets of five. Short repeated
holds build endurance better than one long grinding one.

WHERE IT FITS. Anywhere — a rest day, the end of a session, or the Primer
before a run. It is light enough not to cost you anything the next day.

NOT A TREATMENT. If something already hurts — sharp, radiating, or worse
each time — that is a physiotherapist's job. This plan is for keeping a
history quiet, not for fixing a current injury.

## Prehab · Full (any)

About fifteen minutes. Back first, hips second, shoulders last.

- McGill Curl-up | 3 x 5 (10s holds)
- Side Plank | 5 x 10s/side
- Bird Dog | 3 x 5/side (10s holds)
- Side-lying Hip Abduction | 3 x 12-15/side
- Band Lateral Walk | 2 x 10 steps each way
- Single-leg Glute Bridge | 2 x 10/side
- Eccentric Step-down | 3 x 8/leg
- Band External Rotation | 3 x 15/side
- Band Face Pulls | 2 x 15-20

## Prehab · 8-minute Primer (any)

Before a run or a lifting session — wakes the hips and the trunk without
tiring anything.

- McGill Curl-up | 1 x 5 (10s holds)
- Side Plank | 3 x 10s/side
- Bird Dog | 1 x 5/side
- Band Lateral Walk | 1 x 10 steps each way
- Band External Rotation | 1 x 15/side
