FIVE EXERCISES THE APP CAN COUNT FOR YOU. Every line below is here because
the phone can do the counting while your hands are busy — the tap counter for
reps, the hold timer or a timed interval for the plank. No touching the
screen with your fingers between sets, no losing count at rep fourteen.

HOW THE COUNTING ACTUALLY WORKS. Open the set's counter and the whole screen
becomes one big tap target. Every tap is one rep, spoken out loud as it
lands, and taps closer together than 0.7 seconds are ignored — that window
exists because a face on the way down to the floor hits nose, then chin, then
forehead, and that has to stay ONE rep. Tap Done and the count is written
into the set.

WHERE TO PUT THE PHONE, EXERCISE BY EXERCISE:

- PUSH-UPS — screen up on the floor under your chest. Touch the glass with
  your nose at the bottom of every rep. This is the one the counter was built
  for, and it doubles as a depth gate: no nose, no rep.
- SIT-UPS — screen up on the floor just beyond your feet, or flat on your
  thighs. Tap it with your forehead or chin at the top of each rep.
- PLANK — start the session in TIMED CIRCUIT mode and the 60s below becomes a
  60-second countdown that runs itself. In Reps mode you get a hold timer
  instead: tap once to start, tap again to stop, and it calls out every 15
  seconds while you hold.
- PULL-UPS — phone flat on a shelf, a box or the top of a door frame, at the
  height your chin reaches. Touch it with your chin at the top. If your bar
  has nothing beside it, count these yourself and type the number in — no
  arrangement is worth falling off a bar for.
- SQUATS — screen up on a chair or box behind you, set at your target depth.
  Touch it with your fingertips at the bottom of every rep. Like the push-up,
  the phone becomes the depth standard rather than your judgement at rep
  eighteen.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO: it counts TAPS and TIME, not movement. There is no
camera watching your form and no accelerometer deciding what a rep was. Every
line above works by giving the phone something to be touched by — which is
less clever and considerably more reliable.

ONE ROUND IS BELOW. Go through it two or three times with a minute between
rounds, or use it as a single circuit when time is short. The rep targets are
a starting point; the counter does not care what you planned.

## Counter Circuit (any)

Set the phone down before each exercise rather than carrying it between them
— fumbling with a phone mid-circuit is how the round falls apart.

- Push-ups | 1 x submax
- Sit-Up | 1 x 15-20
- Plank | 1 x 60s
- Pull-ups | 1 x submax
- Bodyweight Squat | 1 x 20
