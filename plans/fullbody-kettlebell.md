One full-body kettlebell session, done when you want one. Not a week, not a
progression — a single workout you can reach for on any day, with one bell and
about half an hour.

It has no weekday, so it sits under `(any)` and fills whatever day nothing
else has claimed: a spare evening, a travel day, the week the real plan fell
over. That is honest about what it is. Pretending it belongs on a Tuesday
would not be.

Legs and the hinge open it, then a long core block through the middle, and
Burpees close it out. Work down the list once, resting only as long as you
actually need. Go around a second time if you have the time and the bell is
light enough to justify it.

ONE BELL IS ENOUGH. If you own two, the core moves want the light one and the
deadlift and the presses want the heavy one — but this was designed around a
single bell and it works that way.
