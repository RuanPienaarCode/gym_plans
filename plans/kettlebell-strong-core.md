STRONG CORE WITH KETTLEBELLS. Difficulty 2 of 3. Pilates mat work with a
kettlebell block in the middle. Knee hugs and the roll-up warm the spine,
the classical mat sequence builds up to the loaded moves, and back extension
(swan, swimming) balances all the forward bending before you finish.

IF YOUR LOWER BACK COMPLAINS, Kettlebell Russian Twist and Kettlebell
Weighted Sit-Up are the moves to leave out first.

HOW TO RUN IT. Start it as a timed session and set the clock for your level:
15 minutes for beginners, 20 for intermediate, 25 for advanced. Each station
gets the default 45 seconds plus a short changeover, and the list repeats
until the time runs out. The rep numbers set the pace; you don't have to hit
them. The order is built so that the first ten stations make a complete
session on their own (the Pilates warm-up and mat sequence and the first kettlebell moves), so a short clock never leaves out a whole
body part. Prefer counting reps? Work down the list once per round instead,
resting as long as you need between rounds.
