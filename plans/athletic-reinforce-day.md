A REINFORCE DAY in the shape a university basketball strength programme
uses: one short activation, then blocks you SUPERSET — two to four
exercises done back to back, rest, round again. It is the lighter day of an
athletic week, and its job is durability: the tendons, ankles, hips and trunk
that let you jump, land, cut and run without breaking down.

READ IT AS BLOCKS. The app lists the exercises in block order; each block is
named at the top of the day. Do one set of every exercise in a block, rest
60–90 seconds, and go round until the block's sets are done. Then the next
block.

`10/side` means ten each side — the original sheet writes it as `10.10`.

WHERE IT FITS. On its own, it is an accessory session with no weekday: start
it from the Plans page on a lighter day, or the day after a hard one. It is
also the Friday of Athletic · Power, Strength & Reinforce, which builds a
whole week in this format.

KIT: a barbell and landmine (or a corner), a light medicine ball, a long band
and a mini band, a kettlebell to hang from the band, and a step. Each
exercise note names a home swap for anything you do not have.

TENDONS ADAPT SLOWER THAN MUSCLE. The tendon and ankle work is meant to feel
easy at the joint and hard in the muscle. Build it over weeks; any sharp,
pinpoint pain at a tendon ends that exercise for the day.

## Reinforce Day (any)

Warm-up · Activate & Stabilise — bear plank to inchworm + t-spine rotation.
Block 1 · OH Strength + Horizontal Upper-body Power — superset.
Block 2 · Trunk + Glute Med + Calves — tri-set. The Pallof press uses a
kettlebell tied to the band, so the load swings.
Block 3 · Tendon Tolerance + Ankle Protection — four in a row. Spanish squat:
your body should look like it is in a wall sit.

- Bear Plank to Inchworm & T-spine Rotation | 2 x 6/side
- Single-arm Landmine Push Press | 3 x 8/arm
- Lying Chest Pass | 3 x 8
- Dynamic Pallof Press | 3 x 10/side
- Captain Morgan | 3 x 12/side
- Single-leg KOT Calf Raise | 3 x 15/side
- Spanish Squat | 3 x 10
- Banded Ankle Dorsiflexion | 3 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Plantar Flexion | 3 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Inversion & Eversion | 3 x 15/side

## Reinforce · Ankle & Tendon Express (any)

Block 3 on its own, about twelve minutes. A band and a step — good after a
run, on a rest day, or anywhere a full session will not fit.

- Spanish Squat | 3 x 10
- Single-leg KOT Calf Raise | 2 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Dorsiflexion | 2 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Plantar Flexion | 2 x 15/side
- Banded Ankle Inversion & Eversion | 2 x 15/side
