KETTLEBELL CORE STRENGTH. Difficulty 2 of 3. Abs, core and back with one
light kettlebell; the core moves are about control, not load. Two standing
moves warm you up, then the floor block runs from the easiest crunch to the
loaded sit-up. Bird Dog finishes it, because a core session needs some work
for the back as well as all that bending forward.

IF YOUR LOWER BACK COMPLAINS, leave out Kettlebell Russian Twist,
Kettlebell Side Twist Standing and Kettlebell Weighted Sit-Up before anything
else. Loaded twisting and loaded sit-ups are the moves a sore back likes
least.

HOW TO RUN IT. Start it as a timed session and set the clock for your level:
10 minutes for beginners, 20 for intermediate, 30 for advanced. Each station
gets the default 45 seconds plus a short changeover, and the list repeats
until the time runs out. The rep numbers set the pace; you don't have to hit
them. A ten-minute clock reaches all eleven stations,
with the last one cut short. Prefer counting reps? Work down the list once per round instead,
resting as long as you need between rounds.

## Kettlebell Core (any)

- Kettlebell Halo | 1 x 5 each way
- Kettlebell Side Twist Standing | 1 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Crunch | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Cross Crunch (Left) | 1 x 8
- Kettlebell Cross Crunch (Right) | 1 x 8
- Kettlebell Double Reach | 1 x 8
- Kettlebell Straight Leg Reach | 1 x 10
- Kettlebell Leg Sway | 1 x 8/side
- Kettlebell Russian Twist | 1 x 10/side
- Kettlebell Weighted Sit-Up | 1 x 8
- Bird Dog | 1 x 8/side
