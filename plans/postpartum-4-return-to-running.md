PHASE 4 OF 4 — RETURN TO RUNNING. Walk-run intervals, built slowly, with the
strength work kept in. The strength is not optional now: it is what keeps the
running symptom-free.

THE PROGRESSION. One step per week at most, and only if the last one was
completely symptom-free. Repeat a week whenever you are unsure.

1. Walk 4 min / jog 1 min  x 5
2. Walk 3 min / jog 2 min  x 5
3. Walk 2 min / jog 3 min  x 5
4. Walk 2 min / jog 4 min  x 4
5. Walk 1 min / jog 5 min  x 4
6. Walk 1 min / jog 9 min  x 3
7. Continuous 20 minutes easy
8. Continuous 25-30 minutes easy

Leaking, heaviness or dragging during or after a run means go back a step and
get it looked at. It is common, it is treatable, and it is not something to
run through.
