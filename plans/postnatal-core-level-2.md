POSTNATAL CORE LEVEL 2. Difficulty 2 of 3. No equipment. The step after
Core Level 1, with planks, side planks and longer levers. The plank moves
alternate with lying-down ones so the wrists and shoulders get a break.
Move up to this level only when Level 1 feels controlled and causes no
symptoms.

BEFORE YOU START: check with your healthcare provider or a pelvic health
physiotherapist that postnatal exercise is safe for you. Breathe out on the
effort with a gentle pelvic floor lift. Stop any exercise that brings on
leaking, heaviness or dragging, pain, or doming up the middle of the belly,
and get it assessed rather than working around it. This plan is general
training guidance, not medical advice.

HOW TO RUN IT. Start it as a timed session and set the clock for your level:
10 minutes for beginners, 15 for intermediate, 20 for advanced. Each station
gets the default 45 seconds plus a short changeover, and the list repeats
until the time runs out. The rep numbers set the pace; you don't have to hit
them. A ten-minute clock reaches all eleven stations,
with the last one cut short. Prefer counting reps? Work down the list once per round instead,
resting as long as you need between rounds.

## Core Level 2 (any)

- Standing Side Bend | 1 x 8/side
- Inch Worms | 1 x 5
- Alternating Push-up Plank | 1 x 6 from the knees to start
- One Leg Circles | 1 x 5/leg
- Plank Hip Rotation | 1 x 6/side
- Pelvic Tilt with Alternating Kick Out | 1 x 6/side
- Side Plank | 1 x 40s from the knees, 20s a side
- Lateral Abductor Crunch | 1 x 8/side
- Kneeling Side Circles | 1 x 8/side
- Inner Thigh Lift | 1 x 10/side
- Snow Angel | 1 x 8
