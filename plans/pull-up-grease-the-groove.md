GREASE THE GROOVE — the classic way to add pull-up reps without adding
fatigue. You do a FEW easy pull-ups MANY times a day, never close to failure,
and the movement gets more efficient the way a skill does. It runs
alongside whatever plan is active rather than replacing it.

THE RULES:

- About HALF your current max per set. If your best is 6, sets of 3. If it
  is 8, sets of 4. Never more than that.
- FIVE sets spread across the day, at least 30 minutes apart — the bar in a
  doorway you walk through is the whole trick. One every hour or two.
- NEVER TO FAILURE, never grinding, never a slow last rep. Every rep should
  look like the first. If a set feels hard, the day is done.
- Four days a week, on days without a heavy pull session. Tue, Thu, Fri and
  Sun keep it off the Monday and Wednesday of a typical pull/push week.

LOG AS YOU GO, OR AT NIGHT. Each line below is the whole day's practice, not
one sitting. Enter the sets whenever is easiest; the app does not mind that
they were hours apart.

RETEST EVERY TWO WEEKS, fresh, at the start of a session — one max set, clean
reps only. Halve the new number and carry on. When the max reaches ten, the
groove is greased; stop this plan and spend the time on the muscle-up.

ELBOWS. Many small sets is a lot of total reps. A dull ache at the inside of
the elbow is the cue to drop a day, not to push through.

## Grease the Groove (tue)

Five sets through the day, half your max each.

- Pull-ups | 5 x half max
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s

## Grease the Groove (thu)

Same again. If Wednesday's pull-ups are still in your arms, do three sets.

- Pull-ups | 5 x half max
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s

## Grease the Groove (fri)

- Pull-ups | 5 x half max
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s

## Grease the Groove (sun)

- Pull-ups | 5 x half max
- Dead Hang | 1 x 30-45s
