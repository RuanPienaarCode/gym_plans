1. Step into a deep lunge, both hands inside the front foot.
2. Drop the back knee slightly, then rotate the inside arm up to the ceiling and follow it with your eyes.
3. Return, swap sides. 5 each side.

**Cue:** earns its name — hips, thoracic spine, hamstrings and ankles in one movement. The single best thing to do before a run or on a rest day.
