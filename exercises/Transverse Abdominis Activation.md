1. On your back, knees bent, fingers just inside your hip bones.
2. Breathe out and gently draw the lower belly in and up, as if zipping up tight jeans — about 30% effort, not a hard brace.
3. Hold for 5 slow breaths while breathing normally. The belly should stay drawn in without the ribs flaring or the back arching.

**Cue:** deep core, not six-pack. Done properly you should still be able to talk. If the belly domes or cones up the midline, ease off — that is too much load for the tissue right now.
