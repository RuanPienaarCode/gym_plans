1. Lie on your side, bottom knee bent for balance, top leg straight and in line with your body.
2. Turn the top toes very slightly DOWN, then lift the leg toward the ceiling, leading with the heel.
3. Stop just above hip height, pause for a second, lower slowly.

**Cue:** this is the glute medius — the muscle that keeps the knee from collapsing inward on every running stride. If you feel it in the front of the hip, the leg has drifted forward; take it slightly back behind you.

Add an ankle weight or a band above the knees once 15 clean reps are easy.
