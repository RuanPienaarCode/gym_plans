1. Sit with knees bent and feet flat, fists at the chest and elbows wide.
2. Lean back a little with a rounded spine and press the arms forward.
3. Sit tall, sweep the arms up and out to the sides in a wide circle, and bring the fists back to the chest.

**Cue:** a Pilates rowing pattern. Move slowly and keep the abs drawn in.
