1. Stand tall, feet together, arms at your sides.
2. Jump the feet out wide and sweep the arms overhead in the same beat.
3. Jump back to the start. Land through the whole foot, not the heels.

**Cue:** the warm-up move that needs no equipment, no space and no thought — which is exactly why it survives every fitness fashion. Two minutes of these raises the heart rate and warms the shoulders before a pressing day. Land quietly; noisy landings mean stiff ankles.
