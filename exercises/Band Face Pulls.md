1. Anchor a band at about face height.
2. Pull toward your face with high elbows, hands finishing beside your ears.
3. Squeeze the rear delts for a beat, then return under control.

**Cue:** with this much pressing, the rear-delt and rotator work is what keeps the shoulders healthy.

Photos show the cable version — a band does the same job.
