1. Lie on your back, arms out wide, knees bent.
2. Let both knees fall slowly to one side while the opposite shoulder stays down.
3. Breathe, 30–60 seconds, then the other side.

**Cue:** gentle rotation for a back that spends the week hanging, pressing and running in one plane.
