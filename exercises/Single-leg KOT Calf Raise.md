1. Stand on one foot on a step or slant board, heel free. Hold something for balance.
2. Bend the knee and push it forward over the toes — and KEEP it there for the whole set (this is the "knees over toes" part).
3. Rise as high as you can onto the ball of the foot, pause, and lower slowly until the heel drops below the step.

**Cue:** a bent knee shifts the work from the gastrocnemius to the soleus — the deeper calf muscle that absorbs most of the load in running and jumping, and that straight-leg calf raises mostly miss. Slow lowering, full range.
