1. Lie on your back, hands light behind your head, shoulders lifted off the floor.
2. Bring one knee in and rotate the opposite shoulder toward it — the elbow follows the shoulder, it does not lead.
3. Switch sides slowly, keeping the lower back pressed into the floor throughout.

**Cue:** the most-rushed exercise in the world. Pulling on your own neck and cycling the legs fast trains nothing; one slow rep with a real rotation beats ten fast ones. A rep is both sides, so 20 is 10 each way.

Photos show it filed as the air bike — same movement, older name.
