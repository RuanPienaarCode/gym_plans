1. Put a mini band around the legs just above the knees (or around the ankles to make it harder).
2. Quarter squat, chest up, feet hip width.
3. Step sideways, keeping tension on the band the whole time — the trailing foot never quite catches up. Count steps each way.

**Cue:** the knees push OUT against the band; do not let them cave. Stay low and do not bob up and down between steps.

The same muscle as side-lying hip abduction, working standing up — closer to what it does when you run.
