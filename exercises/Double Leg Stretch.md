1. Lie on your back, curl the head and shoulders up and hug the knees to the chest.
2. Breathe in and stretch the arms overhead and the legs out at an angle.
3. Breathe out, circle the arms around and hug the knees back in.

**Cue:** the shoulders stay curled up and the back stays down. Legs higher makes it easier.
