1. On your back, arms straight up, knees bent at 90 degrees over the hips.
2. Slowly lower one arm overhead and the OPPOSITE leg toward the floor.
3. Return and swap sides. The lower back stays pressed flat throughout.

**Cue:** the core work to keep when the lower back is grumbling — all the bracing, none of the loading.
