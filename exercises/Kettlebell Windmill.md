1. Bell locked out overhead, feet turned out about 45 degrees away from it.
2. Push the hip under the bell out to the side and hinge down, sliding your free hand down the inside of your leg.
3. Keep your eyes on the bell the whole time; stand back up under control.

**Cue:** thoracic and hamstring mobility under load. Light bell, no rushing — this is the one that keeps a long spine moving well.
