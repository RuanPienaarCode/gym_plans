type: strength
muscles: [glutes, core]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps
---

1. Lie on your side, propped on the forearm. Cross the top leg over and place that foot flat in front of the bottom knee.
2. Lift the straight bottom leg a few centimetres off the floor.
3. Lower with control. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** small and slow. The work is in the inner thigh of the bottom leg.
