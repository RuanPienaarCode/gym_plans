1. Hold one dumbbell or kettlebell against your chest with both hands.
2. Squat down between your knees, chest tall, until the thighs pass parallel.
3. Drive up through mid-foot.

**Cue:** the counterweight out front makes depth easier — it doubles as an ankle and hip mobility drill while still loading the legs.
