1. Stand on one leg, fingertips on a wall for balance only.
2. Rise as high onto the ball of the foot as you can.
3. Lower slowly and fully.

**Cue:** running is a series of single-leg hops, and the calf takes the first hit. 10 good reps a side is another of the return-to-running checks.

Photos show both feet. On one leg the range is the same and the load doubles.
