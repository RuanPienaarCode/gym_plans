1. Clean the bell to the rack in one motion — hips drive it, the arm only guides it.
2. Pause in the rack long enough to be still, then press it strictly overhead.
3. Lower to the rack, then hinge and send the bell back down between the legs for the next rep.

**Cue:** one rep is a clean AND a press, so a set of five is five of each. This is the backbone lift of most classic kettlebell strength programmes because it trains the hinge, the rack position and the overhead press in one movement. Clean each rep fresh rather than pressing five times from a single clean — the two are different exercises.
