1. Bell racked at the RIGHT shoulder, elbow forward and tucked in, ball resting on the back of the forearm.
2. Brace the core and press straight up, as though putting something on a high shelf — wrist stacked over the elbow the whole way.
3. Lower under control back to the rack.

**Cue:** ribs down and no leaning away from the bell — a side bend is not a press. Pairs with Kettlebell Stack The Shelf (Left) — do both, and log them separately.

Photos show the two-bell alternating press — one bell, one side, is the same movement.
