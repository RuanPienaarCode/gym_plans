1. Hands and feet on the floor, hips pushed high into an upside-down V.
2. Bend the elbows to lower the crown of your head toward the floor between your hands.
3. Press back up to straight arms.

**Cue:** the vertical press you otherwise never train. Walk your feet closer — or up onto the box — to make it harder. This is the road to handstand push-ups.
