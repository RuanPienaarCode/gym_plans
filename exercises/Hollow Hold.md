1. Lie on your back and press the lower back FLAT into the floor.
2. Lift shoulders and legs a few inches, arms overhead, so the body makes a shallow dish.
3. Hold that shape and breathe.

**Cue:** the flat lower back IS the exercise — if it arches, raise your legs higher. This is the body line a muscle-up needs.
