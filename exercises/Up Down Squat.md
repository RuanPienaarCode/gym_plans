type: strength
muscles: [quads, glutes]
equipment: bodyweight

1. Feet shoulder width, toes slightly out, arms out in front to balance.
2. Squat to depth and stand straight back up — no pause at the top, no pause at the bottom.
3. Hold that steady up-down rhythm for the whole interval.

**Cue:** the squat run against a clock instead of a rep count, so pace it from the first second. Sprinting ten seconds and then standing there is not the exercise.
