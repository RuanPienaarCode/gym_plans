1. Feet shoulder width, squat to about half depth with the chest up.
2. Jump straight up, driving through the whole foot and swinging the arms.
3. Land SOFT — absorb through the ankles, knees and hips, and sink straight into the next rep.

**Cue:** power without a box or a bar, and the landing is the whole exercise. Sets of 5 to 8 done crisply beat sets of 20 done tired, because a fatigued landing is a sloppy landing. Explosive work asks a lot of tendons, so ramp the volume slowly and skip these entirely on a day the knees are complaining.
