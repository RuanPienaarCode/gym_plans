muscles: [back, shoulders]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps

1. Lie face down with the arms out to the sides like wings, palms down, forehead just off the floor.
2. Lift the chest and arms a few centimetres.
3. Bend the elbows and pull them back toward the ribs, squeezing the shoulder blades, then reach the arms long again before you lower.

**Cue:** the neck stays long and the eyes stay down. The lift is small.
