1. Stand tall with feet hip-width and one arm reaching overhead.
2. Reach up and over to the opposite side, lengthening through the side of the body.
3. Come back to tall and swap sides.

**Cue:** think long and up, not collapsing sideways.

Photos show it holding a dumbbell, which makes it harder.
