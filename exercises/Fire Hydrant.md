equipment: bodyweight

1. On hands and knees, back flat.
2. Keeping the knee bent at 90 degrees, lift one knee out to the side toward hip height.
3. Lower with control. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** the hips stay square to the floor. Leaning away from the lifting leg is the cheat; lift only as high as you can without it.
