1. Mini band around both knees (or ankles). Stand on one leg, slight bend in the standing knee.
2. Lift the other knee to hip height in front of you, then open it OUT to the side against the band — the pose on the rum bottle.
3. Hold a beat, bring it back to the front, lower. Stay tall on the standing leg the whole set.

**Cue:** glute med on BOTH sides at once — the moving leg works against the band, and the standing hip has to stop the pelvis dropping. That standing-side control is what keeps a knee tracking straight on a landing or a running stride.
