1. Lie on your back with knees and hips bent to 90 degrees, holding the bell above your chest.
2. Curl up and reach the bell past your knees.
3. Lower slowly, keeping the legs up.

**Cue:** the lower back stays down. Lighter bell beats a big reach.

Photos show the tuck crunch without a weight.
