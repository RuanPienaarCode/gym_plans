1. Lie on your back, knees bent, the bell held in one hand with the elbow on the floor.
2. Press the bell straight up over the chest until the arm locks.
3. Lower until the elbow touches the floor. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** the wrist stays straight with the bell resting on the back of the forearm.
