type: strength
muscles: [shoulders, core]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps
---

1. Stand with feet hip-width, knees soft and fists by the chin.
2. Punch one arm straight forward at shoulder height, turning slightly through the trunk.
3. Pull it back to the chin as the other arm punches. Keep a quick, steady rhythm.

**Cue:** brace lightly and breathe out on each punch. The ribcage turns, the hips stay steady.
