1. Kick up to a handstand with your heels resting on a wall, hands a little wider than shoulders.
2. Lower under control until your head lightly touches the floor.
3. Press back to a full lockout.

**Cue:** earn these with pike push-ups first. Overhead pressing strength that carries straight into the muscle-up top-out.
