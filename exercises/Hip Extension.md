type: strength
muscles: [glutes, hamstrings]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps

1. Lie face down with the forehead on the hands.
2. Keeping the leg straight, lift it a few centimetres off the floor.
3. Lower with control. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** the hips stay on the floor. Lift from the glute, not the lower back.
