1. Lie on the floor, dumbbells at chest height, knees bent.
2. Press straight up until the arms lock out.
3. Lower until the backs of your upper arms touch the floor, pause, press again.

**Cue:** the bench press you can do without a bench. The floor caps the range, which is easier on a cranky shoulder than a full-depth press.
