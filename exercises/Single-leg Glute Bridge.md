1. On your back, one foot planted, the other leg held straight out.
2. Drive through the planted heel to lift the hips until the body is a straight line.
3. Squeeze the glute at the top, lower slowly, then swap legs.

**Cue:** glute strength without loading the spine — the accessory that helps keep the ITB quiet.
