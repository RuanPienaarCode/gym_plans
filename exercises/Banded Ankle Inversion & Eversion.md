type: strength
muscles: [calves, shins]
equipment: band
unit: reps
---

1. Sit with the leg straight. Anchor a band to the side and loop it around the forefoot.
2. INVERSION: anchor on the outside, turn the sole of the foot inward against the band. EVERSION: anchor on the inside, turn the sole outward.
3. Move only at the ankle — the knee and hip stay still. One rep is in and back (or out and back); do both directions on both feet.

**Cue:** these are the muscles that catch an ankle as it starts to roll on uneven ground — a trail, or someone else's foot. Small range, slow, controlled.
