1. Stand tall, then step one foot BACK into a lunge.
2. Lower until both knees are near 90 degrees, front shin vertical.
3. Drive through the front heel to stand, and alternate.

**Cue:** stepping back is far kinder to the knees than stepping forward.

Photos show the forward/walking version — the bottom position is the same.
