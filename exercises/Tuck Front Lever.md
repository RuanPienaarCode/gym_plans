1. Hang from the bar, then pull the shoulder blades down and back.
2. Tuck the knees to your chest and lift the hips until your back is horizontal.
3. Hold. Straighten the legs a little more as it gets easier.

**Cue:** teaches the straight-arm pulling strength and hollow body that make the muscle-up transition feel less like a fight.
