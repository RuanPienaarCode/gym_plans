1. Feet shoulder width, toes slightly out.
2. Sit down and back, knees tracking over the toes, chest up.
3. Go as deep as you can hold a flat back, then drive up through mid-foot.

**Cue:** the movement everything else is built on, and a fine warm-up or high-rep finisher on its own.
