1. Stand with a light dumbbell in each hand in front of the thighs, palms facing you.
2. Raise the arms straight in front to shoulder height, elbows soft.
3. Lower slowly.

**Cue:** shoulder height and no higher, and no swinging from the hips. If you have to lean back, the weight is too heavy.
