1. Full push-up, body in one line from head to heels.
2. At the top, shift your weight onto one hand and roll into a side plank, the free arm reaching at the ceiling.
3. Come back down to both hands, push-up, rotate the other way. Alternate sides.

**Cue:** hold the top of the rotation for a beat. Whipping straight through it turns the best part of the movement into arm-waving.
