1. Hold the bell(s) at your shoulders and squat to depth.
2. Drive up and let that momentum carry into an overhead press in one motion.
3. Return to the shoulders and go straight into the next rep.

**Cue:** the whole body in one movement — a brutal conditioning finisher where a light 8kg bell is genuinely enough for high reps.
