muscles: [chest, triceps, shoulders]

1. Lie on your back, knees bent, a medicine ball held at the chest.
2. Throw it straight up as HARD and as high as you can — a chest pass to the ceiling.
3. Catch it with soft elbows and absorb it back to the chest, then reset and throw again. Every rep is a fresh, maximal throw.

**Cue:** horizontal upper-body power. Speed is the point, so pick a ball light enough to really throw (3–5 kg). Stop the set when the throws get lower — that is fatigue, not power.

No medicine ball? Explosive push-ups train the same quality.
