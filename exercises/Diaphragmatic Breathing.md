1. Lie on your back, knees bent, one hand on your ribs and one on your belly.
2. Breathe IN through the nose and let the lower ribs widen sideways and back — not just the belly rising.
3. Breathe OUT slowly through pursed lips and feel the ribs soften down.
4. 10 slow breaths.

**Cue:** this is the foundation everything else is built on. The pelvic floor and the diaphragm move together — pelvic floor drops on the in-breath, lifts on the out-breath. Getting that rhythm back is the first job, before any strengthening.
