1. Kneel up, then lean to one side and place that hand on the floor under the shoulder.
2. Straighten the top leg out to the side at hip height.
3. Draw small circles with the leg, then reverse. Swap sides.

**Cue:** the body stays in one long line; the circles come from the hip.
