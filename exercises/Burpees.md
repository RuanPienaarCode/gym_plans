1. Squat down, hands to the floor, jump the feet back to a plank.
2. Chest to the floor (or a push-up), then jump the feet back in.
3. Stand and jump with the hands overhead.

**Cue:** conditioning with no equipment and nowhere to hide. Sets of 10 at an easy pace beat one all-out set — and go easy on these while you are rebuilding after illness.
