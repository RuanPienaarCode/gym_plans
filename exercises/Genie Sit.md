1. Kneel tall with knees hip-width and arms folded in front at shoulder height.
2. Keeping a straight line from knees to head, lean back slowly as far as you can control.
3. Use the thighs and core to return upright.

**Cue:** hinge at the knees, not the hips. Start with a small lean.
