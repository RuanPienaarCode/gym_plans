1. Lie on your back, one knee bent, the other leg straight. Slide your hands, palms down, under the small of your back to keep its natural curve.
2. Brace lightly, then lift the head and shoulders just off the floor as ONE piece — the neck does not bend, the chin does not tuck.
3. Hold 10 seconds, breathing, then lower. Swap the bent leg halfway through the set.

**Cue:** a tiny movement on purpose. The point is ten seconds of the abdominal wall working while the lower spine does not flex at all — endurance for the muscles that hold a back steady, without the repeated bending a sit-up asks for.

One third of the "McGill Big 3", with the side plank and the bird dog.
