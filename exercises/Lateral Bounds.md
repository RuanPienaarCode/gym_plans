1. Stand on one leg. Dip, then push off sideways and jump to land on the OTHER foot, a comfortable distance away.
2. STICK the landing: knee over the middle toes, hip back, hold still for two seconds.
3. Bound back the other way. Each landing is one rep.

**Cue:** lateral power and landing control. The stick is the exercise — distance comes later. If the landing knee caves inward, shorten the bound.
