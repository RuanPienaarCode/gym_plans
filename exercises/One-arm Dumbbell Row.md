1. Hinge forward with one hand braced on the box, back flat.
2. Row the dumbbell to your hip, elbow close, shoulder blade pulling first.
3. Lower all the way to a full stretch.

**Cue:** the loadable row. Inverted rows are limited by bodyweight — this one keeps getting heavier, which is what a growing back needs.
