1. Hinge at the hips with a flat back, free hand braced on a bench, box or your own knee.
2. Pull the bell to the hip, leading with the elbow and letting the shoulder blade slide toward the spine.
3. Lower under control to a full stretch without letting the torso twist up with it.

**Cue:** the torso stays still. If your shoulder is rising and falling to help the arm, the bell is too heavy. Pull to the HIP rather than the armpit — that angle puts the work in the lats instead of the upper trap.
