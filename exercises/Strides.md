1. After an easy run, accelerate smoothly for about 20 seconds to near-sprint — but relaxed.
2. Ease off and walk until fully recovered (about a minute).
3. 4–6 of them. Never a hard effort; the point is turnover, not fatigue.

**Cue:** cheap running economy. Keeps the legs fast while all the other running is deliberately slow.
