1. Lie on your back with a light bell pressed straight up in one hand. Bend the same-side knee.
2. Push off that foot and roll onto your side, then toward your front, keeping the bell arm vertical and your eyes on the bell.
3. Straighten the top leg, breathe for a few seconds, then roll back slowly to the start.

**Cue:** the bell arm stays locked and vertical the whole time. Start very light; this is shoulder stability, not strength.
