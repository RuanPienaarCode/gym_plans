type: strength
muscles: [back, glutes, shoulders]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps

1. Lie face down with arms overhead and legs long.
2. Lift the chest, arms and legs a few centimetres off the floor.
3. Holding the lift, open the arms and legs wide and bring them back together, like a jumping jack.

**Cue:** a low lift is plenty. Squeeze the glutes and keep the neck long, eyes on the floor.
