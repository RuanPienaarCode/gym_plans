1. Hinge forward with a flat back, dumbbell in one hand, upper arm tucked to your side and parallel to the floor.
2. Straighten the elbow until the arm is fully extended behind you.
3. Bend back to 90 degrees. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** only the forearm moves. If the upper arm swings, go lighter.
