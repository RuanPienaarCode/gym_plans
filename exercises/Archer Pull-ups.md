1. Take a wide grip. Pull up toward one hand while the other arm stays nearly straight, guiding.
2. Alternate sides each rep.

**Cue:** the bridge from two-arm pull-ups toward one-arm strength, and serious extra load without extra weight. Only once 8–10 clean pull-ups are yours.

Photos show a standard pull-up. The archer pulls to one side with the other arm straight along the bar.
