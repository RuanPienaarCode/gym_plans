1. Sit with knees bent, feet flat and hands on the floor behind you, fingers pointing forward.
2. Press up until the hips are level with the knees and shoulders: a reverse tabletop.
3. Keeping the hips up, lift one bent knee toward the chest, drawing it slightly across toward the opposite shoulder. Set it down and swap legs.

**Cue:** the hips must not sag when a foot leaves the floor. If they do, just hold the tabletop. Turn the fingers out if the wrists complain.
