1. Hold a band at shoulder width, arms straight out in front of you.
2. Pull the band apart until your arms are wide, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
3. Return slowly under tension — don't let it snap back.

**Cue:** 15 every warm-up. Cheap insurance for the shoulders you press with.
