1. Sit with one leg crossed over the other, that foot flat on the floor.
2. Hug the raised knee across your body and rotate gently toward it.
3. Breathe out into the stretch. 30–60 seconds a side.

**Cue:** the direct answer to a grumbling ITB. Do this one on rest days and after every long trail run — the band itself does not stretch, but the glute and TFL feeding it do.
