1. Hinge forward, back flat, light dumbbells hanging straight down.
2. Raise them out to the sides, squeezing the shoulder blades together.
3. Lower under control.

**Cue:** rear delts — the other half of the shoulder-health work alongside rows and face pulls. Light weight, strict form.

Video: Goulart via wger.de, CC BY-SA 4.0. The bent-over lateral raise is this movement's other name.
