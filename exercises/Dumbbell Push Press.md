1. Stand with the dumbbells at the shoulders.
2. Dip a few centimetres by bending the knees, then drive up through the legs.
3. Use that drive to press the dumbbells overhead to straight arms. Lower to the shoulders and repeat.

**Cue:** the legs start the press and the arms finish it. A short, sharp dip, not a squat.

Photos show it with two kettlebells.
