1. Sit on a chair or box, one foot planted, the other lifted slightly.
2. Stand up using only the planted leg, then lower back down under control.
3. Use a higher seat to make it easier; a lower one to make it harder.

**Cue:** one of the standard return-to-running load tests — 10 clean reps per side, no leaking and no heaviness, is one of the boxes to tick before impact.

Illustration shows the two-legged chair squat. Same movement, same chair — you are doing it on one leg.
