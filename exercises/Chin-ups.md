1. Grip the bar with palms facing YOU, about shoulder width.
2. Pull until your chin clears the bar, elbows driving down.
3. Lower to a full hang every rep.

**Cue:** the supinated grip lets the biceps help, so you will usually get a few more than pull-ups — a good way to add pulling volume on the way to the 10-rep gateway.
