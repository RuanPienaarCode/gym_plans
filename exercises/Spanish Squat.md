1. Loop a heavy band around a solid anchor at knee height and step into it so it sits behind both knees.
2. Walk back until the band is tight. Feet hip width.
3. Sit back into the band until the shins are VERTICAL and the thighs are near parallel — your body should look like it is in a wall sit. Stand back up slowly.

**Cue:** tendon tolerance for the patellar tendon. The band holds the shins upright, so the quads take the load without the knee travelling forward. Slow tempo (3 down, 3 up), or hold the bottom as an isometric for 30–45 seconds. It should feel hard in the quads and fine at the knee.
