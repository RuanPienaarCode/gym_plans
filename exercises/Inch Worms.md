1. Stand tall, hinge over and put your hands on the floor — bend the knees as much as you need to get there.
2. Walk the hands forward until you are in a plank, without letting the hips sag.
3. Walk the feet back in to meet the hands, stand up, repeat.

**Cue:** opens hamstrings and calves and switches the core on in the same rep, which is why it belongs early. Walk it out slowly; hurried ones turn into a flop into plank.
