type: strength
muscles: [shins]
equipment: band
unit: reps
---

1. Sit with the leg straight. Anchor a band in front of you and loop it over the top of the foot.
2. Pull the toes back toward the shin against the band.
3. Return slowly. All reps one foot, then swap.

**Cue:** the front-of-shin muscles that control the foot as it lands. Strong shins are the cheapest insurance against shin splints and rolled ankles.
