type: strength
muscles: [quads, glutes]
equipment: box
unit: reps
---

1. Stand on a low box or step on one leg, the other foot hanging off the side.
2. Bend the standing knee and lower the free heel toward the floor over 3 seconds — knee tracking over the middle toes, hips level.
3. Tap the heel lightly (do not put weight on it) and stand back up.

**Cue:** this is downhill running in slow motion. Quads that are strong while lengthening are what make long descents stop hurting, and a knee that stays over the toes here is one that stays there at kilometre twelve.

Start on a 15–20 cm step; go higher only when the knee stops wobbling.
