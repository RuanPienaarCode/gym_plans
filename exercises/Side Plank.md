1. Lie on your side, elbow directly under the shoulder, feet stacked.
2. Lift the hips so head, hips and heels make one straight line.
3. Hold, then swap sides — time each side separately.

**Cue:** the lateral half of the plank; your plank note already says to sub these in. Good for the hips and the ITB.
