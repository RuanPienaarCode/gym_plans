1. Stand tall holding the bell by the horns at your chest. Holding it overhead is the harder version.
2. Step one knee down to the floor, then the other, so you are kneeling tall. That is down, down.
3. Step one foot forward and stand up through it, then bring the other foot up. That is up, up. Lead with the other leg next rep.

**Cue:** the trunk stays upright the whole way. Pad the knees, and only go overhead if your shoulders are happy there.
