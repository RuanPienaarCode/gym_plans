1. Place one whole foot on the box.
2. Drive through that heel to stand tall on top — let the trailing leg do as little as possible.
3. Step back down under control and alternate.

**Cue:** 10 easy ones in the warm-up prime knees and ankles before jumps.

Photos show the knee-raise version — same step; add the knee drive at the top if you want it.
