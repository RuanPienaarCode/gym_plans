1. On your back, knees bent and feet flat, bell in the LEFT hand pressed straight up, the ball resting on the back of your forearm.
2. Lower back stays pressed into the floor. Tuck the chin, then curl the shoulders up and twist from the core.
3. Push the bell across toward your RIGHT knee, then uncurl slowly, laying the spine down from the bottom up.

**Cue:** the twist comes from the ribs, not from throwing the arm across. Pairs with Kettlebell Cross Crunch (Right) — do both, and log them separately.

Photos show it unweighted. The bell travels the same diagonal.
