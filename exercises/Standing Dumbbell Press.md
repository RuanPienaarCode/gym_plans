1. Stand tall, dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward.
2. Brace the ribs down — no leaning back — and press overhead to a full lockout.
3. Lower under control to the shoulders.

**Cue:** loadable overhead pressing to complement pike and handstand work. Feeds the muscle-up top-out.
