1. Lie or sit comfortably. Breathe out, and gently lift the pelvic floor — the feeling of stopping wind and lifting, not squeezing the glutes or holding your breath.
2. **Long holds:** lift, hold 3-5 seconds building toward 10, then FULLY release. The release matters as much as the lift.
3. **Quick flicks:** 10 fast lift-and-release repetitions.
4. Rest a full breath between efforts.

**Cue:** a pelvic floor that cannot relax is as much a problem as a weak one — never skip the release. If you feel nothing, feel it in the wrong place, or it hurts, that is exactly what a pelvic health physiotherapist is for; guessing here wastes months.
