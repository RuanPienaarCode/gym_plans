1. Start in a bear plank: hands under shoulders, knees under hips and hovering an inch off the floor, back flat.
2. Walk the hands out to a long plank, then walk the feet in toward the hands, legs as straight as the hamstrings allow — the inchworm.
3. Walk the hands back out to plank. Plant one hand, rotate the chest and reach the other arm to the ceiling, eyes following the hand. Return, rotate the other way. Back to bear plank — that is one rep.

**Cue:** activate and stabilise, not a workout. The knees stay an inch off the floor in the bear position and the hips stay level in the plank; the rotation comes from the upper back, not the lower.
