1. On your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width.
2. Breathe out, gently lift the pelvic floor, then press through the heels to lift the hips.
3. Stop where your body makes a straight line from knees to shoulders — do not arch the lower back to go higher.
4. Lower slowly.

**Cue:** the glutes take load off the pelvic floor and the lower back. Pair the lift with the out-breath and the pelvic floor lift; that coordination is the whole point early on.
