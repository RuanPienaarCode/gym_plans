1. Hike the bell back like a one-arm swing and snap the hips hard.
2. As the bell rises, pull the elbow high and back, then punch the hand THROUGH the handle as the bell rotates around it.
3. Catch it locked out overhead with the wrist straight and no bang on the forearm, then guide it back down into the next swing.

**Cue:** the most technical thing in this library, and the one with the most ways to hurt a shoulder or a wrist. Learn it light, learn it from someone who can watch you, and treat a banged forearm as a form fault rather than a rite of passage. Swings and high pulls are a legitimate substitute for every programme that asks for snatches — nothing in your training falls apart if you skip this movement entirely.
