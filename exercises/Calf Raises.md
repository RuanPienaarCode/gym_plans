1. Stand with the balls of your feet on a step, heels hanging free.
2. Rise as high onto your toes as you can.
3. Lower slowly until you feel the stretch at the bottom.

**Cue:** Achilles and calf insurance for box jumps — ramp these BEFORE you ramp jump volume.
