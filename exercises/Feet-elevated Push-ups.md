1. Hands on the floor at shoulder width, feet up on a box, bench or step.
2. Body in one line from head to heels, ribs down — lower until the chest is just off the floor.
3. Press back up without letting the hips sag or pike.

**Cue:** raising the feet shifts the load toward the upper chest and shoulders and makes the push-up meaningfully harder without adding a single kilogram. Start with a low box. The taller you are the longer the lever, so the plank has to hold before the press is worth anything.
