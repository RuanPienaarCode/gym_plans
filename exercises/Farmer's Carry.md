1. Pick up a heavy dumbbell in each hand.
2. Stand tall — shoulders back and down, ribs stacked over hips.
3. Walk a set distance or time without letting the shoulders round forward.

**Cue:** grip strength that feeds straight back into dead hangs, pull-ups and eventually the false grip.
