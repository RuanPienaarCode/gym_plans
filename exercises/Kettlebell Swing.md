1. Hinge at the hips and hike the bell back between your legs.
2. Snap the hips FORWARD to float the bell up to chest height — the arms are just rope.
3. Let it swing back down, hinging again to absorb it.

**Cue:** hips do the work, not the shoulders. The fastest way to train the hinge that powers your box jumps.

Photos show the one-arm version — two hands is the identical hinge.
