1. Sit on the edge of a bench, box or solid chair, hands beside your hips with the fingers over the edge.
2. Slide the hips off the front and lower until the elbows are near 90 degrees, pointing straight back.
3. Press back up to a full lockout.

**Cue:** keep your back brushing the bench — drifting forward moves the work onto the front of the shoulder, and that is the shoulder that ends up sore. Bend the knees to make it easier, straighten the legs to make it harder.
