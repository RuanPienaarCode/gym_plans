1. On hands and knees, back flat.
2. Keeping the knee bent at 90 degrees, press one sole up toward the ceiling until the thigh is level with the back.
3. Lower with control. A dumbbell can be tucked behind the knee to load it.

**Cue:** stop when the thigh is level. Going higher only arches the lower back.
