1. Sit with the knees bent and lean back until your torso and thighs make a V, one dumbbell held at the chest.
2. Rotate the torso to bring the dumbbell down beside one hip, then the other.
3. Chest up, movement in the ribs — the arms only carry the weight across.

**Cue:** a rep is both sides, so 20 is 10 each way. One moderate dumbbell is plenty; this is a rotation drill, not a loading one.

Photos show it unweighted — add the dumbbell and the twist is identical.
