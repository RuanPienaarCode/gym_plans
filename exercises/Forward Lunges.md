1. Stand tall, then step one foot FORWARD into a lunge.
2. Drop straight down until both knees are near 90 degrees, front shin vertical, torso upright.
3. Push back off the front foot to standing, and alternate.

**Cue:** count both legs — 30 means 15 a side. If the front knee objects, the reverse lunge does the same job and is kinder to it.

Photos show the walking version — the step out and the bottom position are the same.
