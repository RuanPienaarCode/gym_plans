1. Stand with feet hip-width, hands behind your head or crossed on your chest, knees soft.
2. Push the hips straight back and let the chest tip forward with a flat back until you feel the hamstrings.
3. Drive the hips forward to stand tall, squeezing the glutes at the top.

**Cue:** it is a hip hinge, not a bow. The back stays flat the whole way; stop where it would start to round.

Photos show it with a barbell. The hinge is the same.
