1. Start in the rack — bell on the forearm, elbow tight to the ribs, wrist straight.
2. Brace the whole body, squeeze the glute on the pressing side, and press the bell straight overhead until the arm locks out beside the ear.
3. Lower it back to the rack under control. That lowering is half the exercise.

**Cue:** this is a strict press, not a push press — nothing bends and rebounds. The bell tracks slightly out and then over as the shoulder rotates; forcing it straight up a vertical line is what pinches. Stop the set on any sharp pain or anything that travels down the arm, and do not press overhead at all on a day the shoulder is already complaining.

Photos show the two-bell version. Pressing one at a time is the same press with more to brace against.
