1. Back flat against a wall, slide down until your knees are at 90 degrees.
2. Thighs parallel to the floor, weight in the heels.
3. Hold and breathe.

**Cue:** quad endurance for long trail descents, with none of the impact. A useful one on days the knees do not want jumping.
