1. Start in a push-up position. Hands on a bench or step makes it easier.
2. As you lower, draw one knee out to the side toward the same-side elbow.
3. Press back up and return the foot. Use the other knee on the next rep.

**Cue:** the hips stay level as the knee travels. Lower only as far as you can keep the body in one line.
