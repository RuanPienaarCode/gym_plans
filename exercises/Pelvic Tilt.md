1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat.
2. Breathe out, gently lift the pelvic floor and tilt the pelvis so the lower back flattens into the floor.
3. Breathe in and release back to neutral.

**Cue:** a small movement driven by the deep abs, not a glute squeeze or a bridge.
