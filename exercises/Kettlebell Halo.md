1. Hold the bell upside down by the horns at chest height.
2. Circle it slowly around your head, close to it, keeping the ribs down.
3. Same number of circles each direction.

**Cue:** shoulder warm-up gold and a perfect job for the 6kg bell. Do these before pressing days.
