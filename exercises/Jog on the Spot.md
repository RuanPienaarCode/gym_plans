1. Stand tall and jog gently on the spot, staying light and quiet.
2. Land softly through the whole foot — no stamping.
3. Stop at the first sign of leaking, heaviness or pain rather than finishing the time.

**Cue:** the first impact your body sees after a long time without any. Treat the target time as a ceiling, not a goal — 30 clean seconds beats a symptomatic minute.
