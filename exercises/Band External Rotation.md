1. Anchor a light band at elbow height. Stand side-on to it, holding the band in the FAR hand.
2. Elbow bent to 90° and pinned to your side — a rolled towel between elbow and ribs keeps it there.
3. Rotate the forearm out away from your belly, keeping the elbow still. Return slowly.

**Cue:** the rotator cuff is small and tires fast — light band, slow reps, no swinging. Every push-up, dip and press trains the opposite direction; this is the cheapest way to keep the joint balanced.

Stop at any pain that travels down the arm.
