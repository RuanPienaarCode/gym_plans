1. Sit with the leg straight and the band looped around the ball of the foot, ends held in your hands.
2. Point the foot away from you against the band, as far as it goes.
3. Return slowly. All reps one foot, then swap.

**Cue:** the push-off direction, through the full range — a lighter, end-range complement to calf raises.
