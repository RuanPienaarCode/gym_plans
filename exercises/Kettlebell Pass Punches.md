1. Stand tall holding a light bell at your chest in one hand.
2. Punch it straight out in front, pass it to the other hand at arm's length, and pull it back to the chest.
3. Punch out again with the new hand and keep alternating.

**Cue:** the ribs stay down and the trunk doesn't twist. Light bell.
