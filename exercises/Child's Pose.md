1. Kneel, sit back onto your heels and reach the arms long in front.
2. Let the chest sink toward the floor and breathe into the back of the ribs.
3. Hold 60 seconds or more.

**Cue:** decompresses the lower back and opens the shoulders. Costs nothing and is a fine way to end any session.
