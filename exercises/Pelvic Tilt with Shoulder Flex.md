1. Set up as for a Pelvic Tilt with arms pointing at the ceiling.
2. Hold the tilt and lower both arms overhead toward the floor.
3. Bring them back up. The ribs and lower back don't move.

**Cue:** lower the arms only as far as the ribs stay down.
