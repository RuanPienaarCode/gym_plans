1. Set up as for a Pelvic Tilt and hold it.
2. Lift one foot and straighten that leg out at a low angle above the floor.
3. Bend it back in, set the foot down and swap legs.

**Cue:** the lower the leg, the harder it is. Keep it high enough that the back stays flat.
