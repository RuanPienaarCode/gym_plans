1. Top of a push-up with a hand on each dumbbell, feet wide for balance.
2. Row one dumbbell to your hip WITHOUT letting the hips rotate.
3. Replace it, then row the other side.

**Cue:** anti-rotation core plus a row in one. The hips staying square is the exercise; if they twist, go lighter.

Photos show the kettlebell version — dumbbells are identical, and a little more stable.
