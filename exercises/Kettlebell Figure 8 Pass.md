1. Stand with feet wide, knees bent, hinged forward with a flat back.
2. Pass the bell between the legs from one hand to the other, around the outside of one leg, then back through to the other hand.
3. Keep weaving it in a figure eight around both legs.

**Cue:** the back stays flat and the hinge stays low. Move the bell, not your spine.
