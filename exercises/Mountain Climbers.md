1. Start in a push-up position, hands under the shoulders, body in one line.
2. Drive one knee toward your chest, then switch legs — the feet swap, the hips do not rise.
3. Keep switching at a pace you could hold for the whole set.

**Cue:** the hips are the tell. As soon as they start bouncing up toward a pike, the exercise has turned into a hip-flexor drill and the core has stopped working. Slow down before that happens. Count both legs, so 40 is 20 a side.
