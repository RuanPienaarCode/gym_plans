1. Warm up 10-15 minutes easy, then 4-6 strides to wake the legs up.
2. Run the hard piece at a pace you could just about hold for 10 minutes — strong, controlled, not a sprint.
3. Jog the recovery, never stand still, and repeat. Finish with 10 minutes easy.

**Cue:** the last repeat should look like the first. If it does not, the first one was too fast — that is the whole skill of interval running. One session a week is plenty; this is the highest-injury-risk run in any plan and the one to skip when a niggle is talking.
