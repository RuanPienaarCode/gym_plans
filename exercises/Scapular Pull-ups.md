1. Hang from the bar with completely straight arms.
2. WITHOUT bending the elbows, pull your shoulder blades down and together so your chest rises an inch or two.
3. Hold a beat at the top, then let the shoulders rise back to a full hang.

**Cue:** already in your warm-up — this is what teaches the lats to switch on before the first real pull-up.
