1. Hands directly under the chest, close together (on dumbbells if your wrists prefer it).
2. Lower with the elbows tracking BACK along your ribs, not flaring out.
3. Press to a full lockout.

**Cue:** triceps-dominant pressing that carries straight into dips and the muscle-up lockout.
