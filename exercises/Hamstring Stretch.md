1. Sit or lie with one leg straight, the other bent out of the way.
2. Hinge from the HIP with a flat back — a rounded spine stretches the back, not the hamstring.
3. 30–60 seconds a side.

**Cue:** flat back is the whole instruction. Especially so with a lower-back history.
