1. Stand tall holding the bell by the horns at your chest, elbows down.
2. Push it straight out at chest height until the arms are nearly straight.
3. Pull it back to the chest, squeezing the shoulder blades together.

**Cue:** ribs down and no leaning back. The core works hardest while the bell is out front.
