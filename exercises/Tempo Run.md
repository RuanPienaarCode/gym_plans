1. Warm up with 10 minutes easy before the tempo starts.
2. Settle into COMFORTABLY HARD — you can speak a short sentence, not hold a conversation.
3. Hold that pace unbroken for the prescribed distance, then jog 10 minutes easy to finish.

**Cue:** the pace people get wrong most often, almost always by running it too fast. A tempo is not a race: if you are gasping by the halfway point, you were 20 seconds a kilometre too quick. Run it by breathing rather than by the watch, especially on a hot day or a hilly route.
