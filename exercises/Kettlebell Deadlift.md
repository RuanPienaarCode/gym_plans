1. Bell on the floor between your feet, feet about hip width.
2. Push the hips BACK and bend the knees to reach the handle — chest up, back flat, weight through the mid-foot.
3. Stand by driving the hips forward, then hinge back down and set it on the floor under control.

**Cue:** this is the swing's hinge at a speed where you can actually feel it going wrong. Own it here before you swing anything heavy.
