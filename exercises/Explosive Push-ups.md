1. Set up as a normal push-up.
2. Lower under control, then push the floor away as FAST as you can, so the hands leave the floor.
3. Land with soft elbows and go straight into the next rep.

**Cue:** upper-body power, not volume. Low reps, full intent on every one. Hands not leaving the floor yet? Do them from an incline (hands on a bench) until they do.
