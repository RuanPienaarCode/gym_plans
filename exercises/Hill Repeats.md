1. Warm up 10 minutes easy.
2. Run hard-but-controlled up a 45–90 second hill; jog or walk all the way down to recover.
3. Start with 4 repeats, build toward 8. Cool down 10 minutes easy.

**Cue:** trail-specific strength — this is what makes mountain climbs stop hurting. Add these only once you have 3–4 weeks of easy running back in the legs.
