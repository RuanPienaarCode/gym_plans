1. On hands and knees, spine long and neutral.
2. Breathe out, gently engage the deep core, then reach one arm forward and the OPPOSITE leg back.
3. Keep the hips level — imagine a glass of water on your lower back.
4. Return with control and swap sides.

**Cue:** anti-rotation core with no pressure down onto the pelvic floor. If the hips wobble or rock, shorten the reach.
