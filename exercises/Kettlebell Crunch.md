1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, holding a light bell at your chest or pressed straight up.
2. Curl the head and shoulders off the floor.
3. Lower slowly.

**Cue:** ribs toward the hips, not chin toward the chest.

Photos show a medicine ball. The bell works the same way.
