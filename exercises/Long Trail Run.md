1. Go slower than your road pace from the first step — trail costs more for the same effort.
2. Walk the steep climbs deliberately. Walking uphill is trail running, not failure.
3. Shorten your stride and look 2–3 metres ahead on technical descents.
4. Take water past about 8km, and something to eat past about 90 minutes.

**Cue:** the weekend run is where the 15km gets built. Time on feet beats pace — never race the ladder.
