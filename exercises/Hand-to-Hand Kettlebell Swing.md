1. Set up for a one-arm swing: hinge, hike the bell back between the legs.
2. Snap the hips forward so the bell floats to chest height.
3. At the top, when the bell is weightless, let go and catch it with the other hand. Swing back and repeat.

**Cue:** learn the two-hand swing first. Swap hands only at the float, never on the way down.

Photos show a one-arm swing. The hand swap happens at the top.
