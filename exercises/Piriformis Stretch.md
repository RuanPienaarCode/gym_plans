1. Lie on your back, ankle crossed over the opposite knee in a figure four.
2. Pull the underneath thigh toward your chest.
3. 30–60 seconds a side.

**Cue:** deep glute. A tight piriformis refers pain into the back and down the leg, so this is worth the two minutes.
