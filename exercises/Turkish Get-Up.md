1. Lie on your back, one arm holding the bell straight up. Never take your eyes off it.
2. Roll to your elbow, then your hand, bridge the hips up, sweep the trailing leg back to a lunge.
3. Stand up. Then reverse every step, slowly, to get back down.

**Cue:** the single best use of a light kettlebell you own — shoulder stability, core control and hip mobility in one 30-second rep. Do them slowly. 6kg is plenty to start.
