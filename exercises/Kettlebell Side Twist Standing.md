1. Stand with feet hip-width, holding the bell by the horns in front of your chest, arms slightly bent.
2. Turn from the ribcage to one side, then the other, with the hips facing forward.
3. Keep it slow and controlled.

**Cue:** the rotation comes from the upper back, not from swinging the bell.

Photos show the bell at arm's length, which is the harder version.
