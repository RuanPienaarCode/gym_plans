1. Anchor a band at chest height and stand side-on to it, feet shoulder width, band held at the chest with both hands.
2. Press the hands straight out in front of the chest and back in, without letting the torso rotate toward the anchor.
3. All reps facing one way, then turn around.

**Cue:** anti-rotation — the trunk's job is to NOT move. The dynamic version hangs a kettlebell from the band (tie it on so it swings freely): the bell bounces with every press, the pull on your hands changes every rep, and the trunk has to react instead of just bracing once.
