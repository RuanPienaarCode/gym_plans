1. Hook the bar with the heel of your palm resting ON TOP of it, wrist bent over the bar — not hanging from the fingers.
2. Thumbs over the bar alongside the fingers. Get set from a box or a jump, then hang with the arms straight.
3. Keep the shoulders active (not shrugged into the ears) and hold. Time it.

**Cue:** the grip the muscle-up transition happens in. With a normal grip the wrist has to roll over the bar mid-rep; with a false grip it is already there. It is uncomfortable on the wrists at first — build the time slowly, and stop at any sharp wrist pain.

A towel over the bar, or chalk, helps while the wrists adapt.
