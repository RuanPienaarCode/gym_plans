1. Lie on your side, legs stacked, bottom forearm on the floor and top hand behind your head. Bend the knees for the beginner version.
2. Lift the top leg and crunch the top elbow toward it at the same time.
3. Lower both slowly. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** lift from the side of the waist; don't pull on the head.

Photos show the straight-leg version.
