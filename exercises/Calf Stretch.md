1. Hands on a wall, one leg back, heel down, that leg straight.
2. Lean in until you feel the upper calf. 30 seconds.
3. Then bend the back knee slightly to shift it to the lower calf and Achilles. 30 seconds more.

**Cue:** both positions matter — the straight-leg version hits the gastroc, the bent-knee one the soleus and Achilles. Non-negotiable once box jumps and hills are in the mix.
