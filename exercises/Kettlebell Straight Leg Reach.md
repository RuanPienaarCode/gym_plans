1. Lie on your back with the legs straight up toward the ceiling, holding a light bell above your chest.
2. Curl the shoulders off the floor and reach the bell toward your toes.
3. Lower the shoulders slowly.

**Cue:** the lower back stays on the floor. If the legs drift away, bend the knees a little.
