1. Lie face down with the forehead resting on the hands.
2. Bend the knees, open them slightly and lift the thighs off the floor.
3. Tap the heels together quickly while holding the lift.

**Cue:** the lift comes from the glutes. Belly button gently in so the lower back doesn't pinch.
