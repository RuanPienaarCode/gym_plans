1. Arms out to the sides, draw slow full circles — small building to large.
2. 10 forward, 10 back, unhurried.

**Cue:** keeps the shoulder moving through its whole range on the days you are not loading it. Given the nerve history, gentle and controlled beats aggressive every time.
