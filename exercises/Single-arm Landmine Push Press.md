1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a landmine (or a corner, wrapped in a towel). Hold the other end at the shoulder in one hand, staggered stance.
2. Dip a few centimetres at the knees, then drive through the legs and press the bar up and forward to a locked elbow.
3. Lower under control back to the shoulder. All reps one arm, then swap.

**Cue:** the angled press path is the shoulder-friendly way to train overhead strength — the arm finishes forward of the ear, not behind it. The legs start the bar; the arm finishes it.

No barbell? A single-arm dumbbell push press with the palm facing in is the closest swap.
