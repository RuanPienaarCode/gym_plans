1. Jump or step into the TOP of a muscle-up: arms locked out, the bar at your hips, false grip.
2. Lower into the bottom of a straight-bar dip, then let the chest roll back under the bar SLOWLY — elbows travel from above the bar to below it.
3. Continue down through a slow pull-up negative to a dead hang. Aim for 5 seconds start to finish.

**Cue:** the transition is the part of the muscle-up nobody can practise by doing more pull-ups. Negatives teach the path the body takes around the bar while you are strong enough to control it. Two good ones beat five ragged ones.

If the shoulder complains at the bottom of the dip, stop the set — that position is the one an old shoulder argues with.
