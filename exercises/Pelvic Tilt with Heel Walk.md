1. Set up as for a Pelvic Tilt and hold the lower back gently flat.
2. Walk the heels away from you in small steps until the legs are nearly straight.
3. Walk them back in, keeping the tilt the whole time.

**Cue:** if the back arches off the floor, you have walked too far out.
