1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hands across the chest or light behind the head.
2. Curl up one vertebra at a time until the torso is off the floor.
3. Lower under control — the lowering is where the work is, and it is the half most people drop.

**Cue:** the classic, and the one with a caveat. Anchoring the feet and yanking through with straight legs turns it into a hip-flexor pull on a rounded lower back. Feet free, knees bent, hands off the neck. If the back objects on any rep, the Hollow Hold and the Dead Bug train the same thing without asking the spine to flex under load.
