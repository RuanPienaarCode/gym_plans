1. Lie on your side, hips stacked, knees bent about 90 degrees, heels together.
2. Keeping the feet touching and the pelvis still, lift the top knee.
3. Lower with control. The top hip must not roll backwards — that is the cheat.

**Cue:** glute medius, the muscle that keeps the pelvis level when you eventually run on one leg at a time. Quiet, unglamorous, and the thing that stops a hip dropping later.
