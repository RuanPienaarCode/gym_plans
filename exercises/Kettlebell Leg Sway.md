1. On your back, arms straight up holding the bell in both hands, shoulders and arms flat on the floor.
2. Lift the legs so the thighs are vertical and the shins horizontal — knees at 90 degrees.
3. Twist the knees slowly to one side, stop before they reach the floor, and pull them back to centre with the core. Alternate.

**Cue:** stopping short of the floor IS the exercise — let them land and you have swapped a core move for a stretch. The bell is an anchor, not something to lift.
