1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight out in front at shoulder height.
2. Pull one elbow back like drawing a bow while the other arm stays reaching forward.
3. Return and pull with the other side.

**Cue:** squeeze the shoulder blade back on every pull and keep the ribs down.
