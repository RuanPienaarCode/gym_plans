1. Lie face down, arms extended in front.
2. Lift chest, arms and legs off the floor and squeeze the glutes.
3. Hold, then lower under control.

**Cue:** the spinal-erector counterweight to all the hanging and pressing. Gentle and controlled — never a thrash.
