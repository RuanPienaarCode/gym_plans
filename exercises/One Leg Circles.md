---
type: mobility
muscles: [core, hamstrings]
equipment: bodyweight
unit: reps
---

1. Lie on your back with one leg straight up to the ceiling and the other long on the floor (or bent if the hamstrings are tight).
2. Draw circles with the raised leg, crossing the body and around.
3. Reverse direction, then swap legs.

**Cue:** the pelvis stays still. Keep the circles small enough that it does.
