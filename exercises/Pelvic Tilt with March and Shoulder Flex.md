1. Set up as for a Pelvic Tilt with arms pointing at the ceiling.
2. Breathe out, lift one knee over the hip and lower the opposite arm overhead.
3. Return and swap sides.

**Cue:** slow and smooth. If the back lifts or the belly domes, go back to the march alone.
