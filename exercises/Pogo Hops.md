1. Stand tall, feet hip width, arms relaxed.
2. Hop continuously using ONLY the ankles — knees almost straight, heels never touching down.
3. Short, quick, springy contacts. Count every landing.

**Cue:** ankle stiffness — the spring that returns energy on every stride and jump. Quiet landings, minimum time on the floor. Start with a couple of sets of 15; they are harder on the Achilles than they look.
