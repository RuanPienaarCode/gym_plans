1. On your back, knees bent, deep core gently engaged.
2. Breathe out and slide one heel along the floor until the leg is straight, keeping the lower back still.
3. Slide it back and swap sides.

**Cue:** the first real test of whether the deep core can hold the spine steady while a limb moves. Only go as far as you can go without the back arching off the floor.
