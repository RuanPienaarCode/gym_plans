1. Jump or step up so your chin starts above the bar.
2. Lower yourself as SLOWLY as you can — aim for 5 seconds, work toward 8–10.
3. Step back up; do not try to pull yourself up.

**Cue:** the highest-value pull-up builder there is. When reps stall, negatives are what break the plateau — you are strong on the lowering long before the lifting.

Photos show a full pull-up; the negative is the lowering half of it, slowed down.
