1. Clean the bell into the rack, elbow tucked in, wrist straight.
2. Sit down between the hips — knees track over the toes, chest stays up, the bell never drifts forward.
3. Drive back up through the whole foot without letting the elbow come off the ribs.

**Cue:** the rack is the hard part, not the legs. Holding a bell in front means the torso has to stay upright, which is exactly why this squat is kinder to a long-levered back than a loaded squat with the weight behind you. Breathe at the top, not at the bottom.
