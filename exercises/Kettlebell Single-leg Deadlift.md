1. Stand on one leg holding the bell in the opposite hand, standing knee soft.
2. Hinge forward, letting the free leg float back in line with the body, until you feel the hamstring.
3. Drive the hips forward to stand tall. Do all reps on one side, then the other.

**Cue:** hips stay square to the floor; the free leg's toes point down. Touch a wall for balance if you need it.
