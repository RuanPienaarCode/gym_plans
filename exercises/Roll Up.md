1. Lie on your back with legs long and arms overhead.
2. Bring the arms forward and peel the spine off the floor one vertebra at a time until you reach toward the toes.
3. Roll back down just as slowly.

**Cue:** no momentum. Bend the knees if you can't roll up smoothly.
