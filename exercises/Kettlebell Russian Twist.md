1. Sit with the knees bent and lean back until your torso and thighs make a V, bell held at the chest.
2. Rotate the torso to bring the bell down beside one hip, then the other.
3. Chest stays up and the movement stays in the ribs — the arms just carry the bell.

**Cue:** a rep is both sides. Feet on the floor is a real version of this, not a soft one; lifting them is harder, not better.

Photos show it unweighted — add the bell and the twist is identical.
