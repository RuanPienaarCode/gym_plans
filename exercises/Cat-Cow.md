1. On hands and knees, slowly round the spine toward the ceiling.
2. Then reverse: let the belly drop and the chest open.
3. Move with your breath, 8–10 slow cycles.

**Cue:** gentle spinal movement with no load — a good first thing on any morning the lower back feels stiff.
