1. Hang from the bar, shoulders engaged, body still.
2. Raise both knees toward your chest without swinging.
3. Lower slowly all the way down.

**Cue:** rung one of the toes-to-bar ladder. Own this cleanly before you straighten the legs.

Photos show the straight-leg version. Bent knees are the same movement with a shorter lever.
