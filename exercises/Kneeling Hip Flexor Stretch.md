1. Half-kneel, back knee down, front foot flat.
2. Tuck the tailbone under (this is the bit people skip) and shift gently forward.
3. You should feel it at the front of the back hip, not the lower back. 30–45 seconds a side.

**Cue:** running and desk-sitting both shorten these, and tight hip flexors are a common driver of lower-back complaints.
